A brief, incomplete history of the BBS:
  1978  Ward & Randy fire up CBBS in a Chicago snowstorm.
  1984  FidoNet stitches the boards together over midnight long-distance.
  1991  Door games (LORD, TradeWars) eat everyone's evenings.
  1994  TriBBS 5.01. This is the one we're rebuilding.
  1995+ The web arrives; the boards quiet but never fully die.
  now   You're reading this on a clone that reads the real DAT files.
