                         Welcome to TresBBS!

  A spiritual successor to TriBBS, running on modern iron. You dialed in
  over telnet, but the soul is pure 1994: @VARIABLE screens, message
  conferences, file areas, doors, and multinode chat.

  Type your way around from the main menu. New files land in the file
  areas; leave a message in a conference; page another node for a chat.

  - The Management
